# Sources

## Industry design and AI references

- [Make me think, make me move: DOOM's deceptively simple design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/-make-me-think-make-me-move-new-i-doom-i-s-deceptively-simple-design) — combat chess, enemy individuality, readable information and player movement.
- [Cyber Demons: The AI of DOOM (2016)](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-) — attack permission, movement-aware accuracy and AI serving the push-forward combat pillars.
- [Enemy design and enemy AI for melee combat systems](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/enemy-design-and-enemy-ai-for-melee-combat-systems) — interviews with combat developers on tells, attack pacing, grouping and enemy purpose.
- [Enemy Attacks and Telegraphing](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/enemy-attacks-and-telegraphing) — readable questions, avoidable damage and attack communication.
- [Three States and a Plan: The AI of F.E.A.R.](https://gdcvault.com/play/1013459/Three-States-and-a-Plan) — planning creates convincing tactical behavior from a compact state structure.
- [AI in the Awesomepocalypse: Creating the Enemies of Sunset Overdrive](https://gdcvault.com/play/1021780/AI-in-the-Awesomepocalypse-Creating) — enemy AI built around movement-heavy player experience.
- [Enemy design](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/combat/enemy) from The Level Design Book — roster roles and orthogonal differentiation.
- [Anatomy of an Enemy Attack in Dark Souls 3](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/anatomy-of-an-enemy-attack-in-dark-souls-3) — perception, reaction and the timing chain of readable enemy attacks.

## Official Godot documentation

- [Navigation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/navigation/index.html) — maps, regions, paths, agents, obstacles, layers and optimization.
- [Using NavigationAgents](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/navigation/navigation_using_navigationagents.html) — path following and avoidance workflow.
- [NavigationAgent2D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_navigationagent2d.html) — required physics-frame path updates and avoidance cost.
- [Using AnimationTree](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/animation/animation_tree.html) — advanced animation transitions and state machines.

Enemy AI quality means creating understandable, expressive opposition. Do not equate it with maximizing computational intelligence or defeating the player efficiently.
