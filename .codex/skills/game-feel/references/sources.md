# Sources

## Foundational and research sources

- [Game Feel: The Secret Ingredient](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-feel-the-secret-ingredient) by Steve Swink — real-time control, simulated space and polish as a unified tactile experience.
- [Principles of Virtual Sensation](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/principles-of-virtual-sensation) by Steve Swink — control, mastery and the perceptual chain between input and response.
- [Good Game Feel: An Empirically Grounded Framework for Juicy Design](https://doi.org/10.26503/dl.v2018i1.936), DiGRA 2018 — industry-informed framework for analyzing juiciness rather than treating it as an undefined quantity.
- [Juice it or lose it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg) by Martin Jonasson and Petri Purho — influential demonstration of layered feedback transforming a simple prototype.
- [The Art of Screenshake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U) by Jan Willem Nijman — practical examples of impact feedback and exaggeration.
- [Cornell Gamefeel Critique](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs5152/2023sp/assignments/critique4/) — structured playtest prompts focused on animation, sound and input responsiveness.
- [Enemy Attacks and Telegraphing](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/enemy-attacks-and-telegraphing) — feedback as readable gameplay information, especially for avoidable damage.

## Official Godot documentation

- [2D features](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/index.html) — particles, animation, custom drawing, lighting and parallax.
- [Animation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/animation/index.html) — AnimationPlayer, AnimationTree and animation workflows.
- [Audio buses](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/audio/audio_buses.html) — routing, effects and mix organization.
- [Audio features](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/list_of_features.html#audio) — positional audio, random sample selection, pitch variation and polyphony.
- [Fixing jitter, stutter and input lag](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/rendering/jitter_stutter.html) — diagnose responsiveness problems before adding presentation effects.

Use these sources as principles, not immutable numeric prescriptions. Validate every effect in the actual game, camera scale, frame rate and target device.
