# Sources

## Level and world design

- [The Level Design Book](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/) — open, critical and production-oriented level design reference.
- [What is level design](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/introduction) — distinguish room, level and world design; world design focuses on flow and wayfinding at larger scales.
- [Critical path](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/layout/criticalpath) — mark minimum progression route while expecting real players to wander.
- [Wayfinding](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/wayfinding) — player goals and context determine how environmental cues work.
- [Flow](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/layout/flow) — layout, circulation, path shape and movement rhythm.
- [Pacing](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/preproduction/pacing) — explore, combat, choreo and puzzle beats; nonlinear worlds rely more on zones and metrics.
- [Sparking Curiosity-Driven Exploration](https://media.gdcvault.com/GDC%2B2021/beachum_gdc_2021%281%29.pdf), GDC 2021 — Outer Wilds design goals, curiosity, information and world structure.
- [Design Guidelines for Landmarks to Support Navigation in Virtual Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0304001) — empirically grounded landmark selection and placement guidance.

## Official Godot documentation

- [Using TileMaps](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/using_tilemaps.html) — TileMapLayer organization, rendering quadrants, physics and navigation limitations.
- [Using TileSets](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/using_tilesets.html) — atlas sources, terrains, physics, navigation and custom data layers.
- [2D navigation overview](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/navigation/navigation_introduction_2d.html) — maps, regions, agents, obstacles and links.
- [Using navigation meshes](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/navigation/navigation_using_navigationmeshes.html) — source geometry parsing and baking.
- [CanvasItem `y_sort_enabled`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_canvasitem.html#class-canvasitem-property-y-sort-enabled) — top-down rendering order and nested y-sort behavior.

Treat maps and pacing documents as testable hypotheses. Blockout playtests take precedence over an attractive but unverified plan.
